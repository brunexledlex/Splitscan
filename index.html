Ditongo

I used to have an iOS app on my iPod Touch to do slitscans. I had a lot of fun with it. This experimental slitscan webapp is a testing ground where I'm testing different ways to explore the same principles, stretching more beyond time and photography.

Single purpose apps for photography bore me, and I didn't want to install an app to use it every now and then, so i made it into a web app that works locally. Slitscans stay on your app roll until you download or delete them.
